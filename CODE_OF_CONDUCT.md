# Code of Conduct

## Our standard

Be respectful, constructive, and specific. Welcome people of different backgrounds and experience
levels. Discuss ideas and code without attacking the person behind them.

Harassment, threats, discrimination, sexualized conduct, deliberate intimidation, doxxing, and
publication of another person's credentials or private information are not acceptable.

## Project spaces

This policy applies to issues, pull requests, discussions, reviews, and other spaces used to
represent XTrawl. Technical disagreement is expected; personal abuse is not.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit or remove content, close contributions, issue warnings, or temporarily or
permanently restrict participation when conduct harms the project or its community. Enforcement will
consider context, severity, and repeated behavior.

To report conduct privately, contact the repository owner through the contact methods listed on the
[ensp1re GitHub profile](https://github.com/ensp1re). Do not put sensitive personal information in a
public issue.
